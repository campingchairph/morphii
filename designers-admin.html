Orders

Sticker sheet orders

Customers pay on Shopee and send their order number in chat. Open layout loads the sheets so you can download the print PNG and cut-line SVG. For phone orders, Send text opens your messaging app with the confirmation already written.

Button pin minimum order (cut strip)

A4 is cut lengthwise into one strip; the customer must complete every circle on it before checking out. Set how many pins fit per size, how they are arranged, and the strip width (mm). Defaults: 25mm = 14 (2 columns), 32mm = 7 (zigzag), 58mm = 4.

Sticker pricing

Price per A4 sheet, cutting included. Customers below the minimum get a big “minimum sheets per order” prompt and can’t check out. The tier with the biggest “from” that the sheet count reaches sets the price per sheet — use a high “from” (e.g. 50) for the discounted bulk price. Customers see the total only on the final review screen.

Stickers waiting for review

Approve only original work. Approved stickers appear in the Premium Library right away (free-tier uploads always; paid-tier uploads while the designer’s pass is active).

Pass payments to confirm

Check the GCash reference against your GCash history first. Confirming gives the designer the paid slots for 30 more days and relists their paid stickers. ₱1 = 1 slot.
