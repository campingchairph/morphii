What are button pins?

A button pin — also called a pinback button, pin button or badge — is a round printed disc with a metal pin on the back. You clip it on a bag, jacket, lanyard or uniform. Because the artwork is printed edge to edge, button pins are one of the most affordable ways to turn a design, a photo or a message into something people actually wear.

What can you put on a custom pin?

  • Photos — a portrait, a couple shot, a pet, a group picture.
  • Stickers and shapes — pick from the Morphii library or upload your own PNG with a transparent background.
  • Text and word art — names, dates, hashtags, slogans, in a choice of fonts.
  • Borders and backgrounds — frame the pin so it looks finished from across the room.

Which size should you choose?

Smaller pins (25–37mm) suit simple logos and initials. The popular 44mm and 58mm sizes have room for a face or a short phrase, and 75mm makes a bold statement piece. See the full button pin size guide for each size in millimetres and inches.
