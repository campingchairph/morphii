Button pins for schools, organizations and events

Give every member, batch or participant a pin that shows they belong. Custom button pins work for student orgs, class batches, school events, seminars and company activities.

Start designing — it’s free

Where organizations use button pins

  • Student orgs and batches — membership pins, batch year pins, graduation keepsakes.
  • School events — intramurals, foundation day, acquaintance parties, club fairs.
  • Seminars and conferences — pins that double as simple name or role badges.
  • Companies and teams — anniversaries, outreach drives, fun-run participants.

Make your logo look sharp

Upload your logo as a PNG with a transparent background so it sits cleanly on a coloured pin. Keep two or three brand colours and use the logo large — small details disappear at pin size. The designer lets you add a border in your colours and a line of text such as the year or event name.

Which size?

32–44mm suits a logo with a short word. 58mm leaves room for the batch year or event name. Compare in the size guide.

Group orders

Agree on one final design with your officers before submitting, confirm the number of pins you need, and place one order for the whole group — it keeps everyone’s pins identical. Read how ordering custom button pins works, or start designing now.

Frequently asked questions

Can we use our school or organization logo?

Yes. Upload your logo as a PNG (a transparent background works best) and place it on the pin, then add text and a border.

How should a group order be handled?

Choose one final design, confirm the total number of pins your group needs, and place a single order so all pins match.

How do I get my order confirmed?

Submit the design to receive your save code, pay on the Morphii Shopee store and send the code in the Shopee chat.
