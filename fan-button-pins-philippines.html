Fan button pins: custom badges for your favourite artists, shows and teams

Fan pins are a fandom classic — bias badges, group pins, concert-day buttons and fan-meeting souvenirs. Build yours online with layered stickers, photos and text.

Start designing — it’s free

Popular fan pin styles

  • Bias badge — one cropped face, bold name, a sticker or two for sparkle.
  • Group pin — a collage of the whole group or cast.
  • Lightstick colours — a border or background in the fandom’s colours.
  • Event pins — concert date and venue, fan-meeting or watch-party souvenirs.
  • Team and fandom group pins — your fan club’s logo, made in bulk for a meet-up.

Layering that looks good

Start with a background colour, add the photo, then use stickers and shapes to frame it. Keep the face and name away from the edge so nothing is lost at the cut line. Text works best in two sizes: a big name and a small date.

Which size?

44mm is a classic bias-badge size; 58mm fits a bigger crop and a message; 75mm suits collages. See the size guide.

Use artwork you have the right to

Use your own photos and edits, original fan art you made or have permission to use, and designs from the Morphii library. Please do not print artwork that belongs to someone else without their permission.

Frequently asked questions

What size is best for a fan pin?

44mm is a classic badge size, 58mm gives more room for a bigger crop and text, and 75mm works for collages.

Can I add stickers over my photo?

Yes. You can layer stickers, shapes and text over the photo and move, resize and rotate each one.

How do I order a group of fan pins?

Submit one final design, pay on the Morphii Shopee store, and send your save code in the Shopee chat.
