Memorial button pins: a keepsake to remember a loved one

A memorial pin is a small, personal way for family and friends to carry someone with them. Add their photo, name and dates, and share the pins at the wake, a memorial service or an anniversary.

Start designing — it’s free

What goes on a memorial pin

  • A clear portrait of your loved one.
  • Their name, and the years — for example 1950 – 2026.
  • A short line such as “In loving memory” or a favourite phrase.
  • A simple border or soft colour that suits them.

Choosing the photo

A close, well-lit photo with the face clearly visible works best. If the original is small or old, scan it at a higher resolution when you can. Plain backgrounds make the face easier to read at pin size. The designer lets you crop and position the photo before adding text.

Which size?

58mm gives enough room for a face, a name and the dates without crowding. 44mm is a good lighter option. Compare them in the size guide.

When families give memorial pins

  • At the wake or funeral, as a keepsake for visitors.
  • For a 40th-day or first-anniversary memorial.
  • For family reunions where relatives travel to remember together.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use an old or scanned photograph?

Yes. Upload the best-quality version you have and crop it so the face is large and clear in the pin.

What size works best for memorial pins?

58mm is comfortable for a face plus a name and dates; 44mm is a lighter option.
