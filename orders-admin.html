❓ Getting Started

What this page is

Every design a customer finishes in the pin builder shows up here as an order. This page is where you review, approve, and print them.

The usual flow for one order

  • Pending — a new submission, not reviewed yet.
  • Approve or Reject it based on whether the design is print-ready (readable, nothing offensive, fits the cut line).
  • Once approved, check its box and send it to 🖨 Layout & Print — that opens a tool that auto-arranges it (and any others you've checked) onto a paper sheet at the correct physical size, ready to download as a print-ready PDF.
  • After it's physically printed, tap 🖨 Queue → ✓ Printed on the card so it's marked done.

What each card button does

  • Approve / Reject — sets the order's status.
  • 🖨 Queue / ✓ Printed — toggles whether this order has been physically printed.
  • ⬇ — downloads a clean, full-resolution copy of the design (no watermark) as a PNG.
  • 🗑 — permanently deletes that one order. There's no undo.
  • Tapping the checkbox in a card's corner selects it for a batch action (Layout & Print, or Delete — see the "Delete" button near the top once you've selected orders).

Finding orders

Use the search bar (code, email, or name), the product tabs, the status dropdown, and the date range dropdown together — they all combine. The sort dropdown flips between newest-first and oldest-first.

The other buttons up top

  • 📊 Visitors — how many unique devices have opened the pin builder, and roughly where from.
  • ⚰️ Funeral Calc — mixed-size order calculator with A4 page-fill visuals and referral/wholesale commission math for funeral home partners.
  • 💰 Finance — track costs, set pricing per product/size, and see margin/ROI, unrelated to individual orders.
  • 📦 Catalog — turn a product or size on/off shop-wide, e.g. when you're out of stock, no code changes needed.
  • 🖼 Assets — see every sticker/border/background/etc. available in the builder and rename how it's labeled.
  • 🔤 Fonts — add a Google Font by pasting its link; it's instantly available to customers in the text tool.
