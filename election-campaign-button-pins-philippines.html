Campaign button pins for election season in the Philippines

Button pins keep a candidate’s name, number and message visible all day. Design your campaign pin online and order enough for your volunteers and supporters.

Start designing — it’s free

What a good campaign pin includes

  • Name and position in large, high-contrast letters.
  • Ballot number or slogan — one clear message beats three small ones.
  • A friendly photo if it helps recognition, cropped tightly.
  • Party or team colours so supporters spot each other.

Design for readability

People read pins from a distance, in crowds and in motion. Use thick fonts, strong contrast between text and background, and keep the wording short. At 58mm there is room for a face plus a name; at 75mm you can add a slogan. See the size guide to compare.

Before you print

  • Check the current COMELEC guidelines for campaign materials in your area so your pins comply.
  • Have the final name spelling and number approved by the team before submitting.
  • Plan quantities for volunteers, rallies and house-to-house teams, and order early — campaign periods are busy.

Frequently asked questions

What size is best for campaign pins?

58mm is a popular choice: large enough for a photo with a name. 75mm leaves space for a slogan too.

Can I add a photo and ballot number?

Yes. Upload a photo and add the name, number and slogan with the text tools.
