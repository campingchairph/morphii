Button pin size guide: 25mm to 75mm (with inches)

Choosing the right size is the first decision when you make custom button pins. Here is every Morphii pin size in millimetres and inches, and what fits comfortably on each.

How to pick

Ask what the pin has to show

A single word or logo reads well even at 25–32mm. A face needs at least 44mm so the eyes do not turn into dots. Anything with two or three lines of text looks better at 58mm and up.

Think about where it will be seen

Pins worn on a jacket are viewed from a metre or two away, so bolder shapes and bigger text help. Pins held in the hand — wedding souvenirs, event giveaways — can carry finer detail.

Match the size to the occasion

For a crowd, 44mm and 58mm are the sizes people tend to enjoy wearing. Larger 75mm pins feel special but are heavier, so they are better for people who will clip them to a bag.

Frequently asked questions

What is the most popular button pin size?

44mm and 58mm are the sizes most people choose: big enough for a photo or a short phrase, small enough to wear comfortably.

What size is 58mm in inches?

58mm is about 2¼ inches.

Can I print a full-colour photo on a button pin?

Yes. The designer lets you upload a photo, crop and position it, and add text and stickers over it.
