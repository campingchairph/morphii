Why pins make good wedding souvenirs

Button pins are light, easy to hand out at a reception table and genuinely wearable — on a bag, a jacket or a lanyard. Unlike a magnet or card that ends up in a drawer, a pin goes back out with your guests.

Design ideas

  • Monogram and date — your initials in the centre with the wedding date underneath.
  • Pre-nup or favourite photo — a photo crop with your names in a script font.
  • Hashtag pin — your wedding hashtag in bold letters; guests can pin it on during the party.
  • Entourage pins — a different colour or border for the entourage, family and sponsors.
  • Motif match — pick the borders and sticker colours that match your wedding theme.

Which size?

44mm is a neat everyday size for a monogram. 58mm gives room for a photo and names together. See the size guide for all options.

Planning tips

  • Count your guests, then add some extra for the entourage, suppliers and last-minute additions.
  • Finalise names and dates before you submit — check spelling twice.
  • Use a clear, well-lit photo; blurry photos look blurrier when printed small.
  • Order early so there is time for delivery before the wedding.

Frequently asked questions

How many giveaway pins should I order for my wedding?

A simple rule is one per guest plus a few extra for the entourage, suppliers and surprises. Counting your final guest list first avoids running short.

Can I put our photo and wedding date on the pin?

Yes. Upload a photo, then add your names, date or hashtag with text tools and stickers.
